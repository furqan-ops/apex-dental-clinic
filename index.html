Frequently Addressed Questions

What does the complimentary initial consultation include?

The session includes a full panoramic 3D intraoral digital capture, periodontal diagnostic review, and a direct consultation with our lead dentist to discuss suitable clinical protocols.

What are the clinic's weekend operating hours?

We operate on Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We remain closed on Sundays. Our automated front desk platform accepts online reservations 24 hours a day.

What protocol is followed for emergency clinical triage?

Inquiries concerning active hemorrhaging or trauma bypass automated workflows and generate high-priority notifications to our on-call clinical staff.
